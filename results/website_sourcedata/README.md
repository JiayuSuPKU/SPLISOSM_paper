## Collection of processed isoform-level spatial transcriptomics datasets of mouse and human brain
This data repository contains processed spatial transcriptomics datasets generated by various platforms (10X Visium-SR, Visium-ONT, Xenium Prime 5K) with isoform-level quantification. For long-read datasets, isoform quantification was performed by the original publications. For short-read datasets, we applied Sierra to extract and quantify transcript 3'end diversity (TREND) events. Spatial variable isoform usage and differential usage tests were performed using [SPLISOSM](https://github.com/JiayuSuPKU/SPLISOSM). See our associated [preprint]() and GitHub [repository](https://github.com/JiayuSuPKU/SPLISOSM_paper) for more details.

Reference: Su, Jiayu, et al. "A computational framework for mapping isoform landscape and regulatory mechanisms from spatial transcriptomics data." bioRxiv (2025): 2025-05.

### Sample summary
* Mouse CBS (2+1+1=4):
  * 2 Visium-ONT samples of coronal brain section (CBS1, CBS2), healthy adult
  * 1 Visium-SR sample of coronal brain section (CBS), healthy adult
  * 1 Xenium Prime 5K dataset of coronal brain section (CBS), healthy adult
* Human brain (12+13+11=36):
  * 12 Visium-SR samples of postmortem healthy human dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) samples (151507, 151508, 151509, 151510, 151669, 151670, 151671, 151672, 151673, 151674, 151675, 151676)
  * 13 Visium-SR samples of surgically removed human glioma samples (MGH258, ZH881inf, ZH881T1, ZH916bulk, ZH916inf, ZH916T1, ZH1007inf, ZH1007nec, ZH1019inf, ZH1019T1, ZH8811Abulk, ZH8811Bbulk, ZH8812bulk)
  * 11 Visium-ONT samples of surgically removed human glioma samples (DMG_1, DMG_2, DMG_3, DMG_4, DMG_5, GBM_1, GBM_2, GBM_3, GBM_4, GBM_5, GBM_6)

### Repository structure
```
.
├── human_dlpfc
├── human_glioma_ont
├── human_glioma_sr
├── mouse_cbs
├── README.md
├── sp_tx_diversity_human.ipynb
├── sp_tx_diversity_mouse_cbs.ipynb
└── splisosm_test_results
```  
* `sp_tx_diversity_mouse_cbs.ipynb` and `sp_tx_diversity_human.ipynb` are the interactive notebooks for visualizing mouse and human datasets in this repository. They are also available via Google Colab.
* `splisosm_test_results` contains spatial variability and differential usage test results (dataframes) for all samples (40 + 1 Slide-seqV2 adult mouse hippocampus).
* `mouse_cbs`, `human_dlpfc`, `human_glioma_ont`, and `human_glioma_sr` each contain per-sample results for the respective datasets. 

Each sample folder contains the following files:
  1. `iso.quant.h5ad`: isoform-level quantification, has data layers 'counts', 'log1p', 'ratios_obs', 'ratios_smoothed'.
  2. `gene.quant.h5ad`: gene-level quantification, has data layers 'counts', 'log1p', and additional spot annotations in adata.obs.
  3. `sv_results.csv`: results of spatial variability tests. All genes included in `iso.quant.h5ad` are tested for spatial variability.

For samples where we ran differential usage tests, a `du_results.csv` file is also included. Note only a subset of spatially variable genes and RBPs are tested.

  4. `du_results.csv`: results of differential usage tests.

Depending on the dataset, we also provide the detailed structure of spatially variably used transcript events, either full-length isoforms (GTF) or local TREND region (BED12).

  5. `svs.iso.gtf` (for ONT samples): isoform structure of spatially variably processed genes, which is a subset of genes in `iso.quant.h5ad` and `sv_results.csv`, filtered based on 'padj_hsic-ir'. For mouse SiT datasets, the GTF is created from the Mus_musculus.GRCm38.102 reference annotation. For human ONT glioma datasets, it contains both known isoforms from Human Gencode v32 (hg38) reference annotation and novel isoforms identified in the study.
  5. `svs.exon.bed` (for SR samples): TREND structure of spatially variably processed genes in BED12 format, based on mm10 (mouse) and hg38 (human) genome assembly.
